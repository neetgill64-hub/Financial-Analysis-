# Financial Data Analysis & Business Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyses financial transaction data to evaluate business performance across customers, products, departments and regions.

The analysis uses SQL in Google BigQuery and Power BI to transform raw financial data into actionable business insights.

## Business Problem

The business wants to understand:

- Which customers generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which customer segments deserve more investment?
- Which customers are becoming less active?
- Which customers may be at risk of churn?
- Which products generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which departments are performing above or below budget?
- Which regions and cities are performing best?
- Where are the biggest opportunities for improvement?

## Objectives

- Analyse revenue and profitability
- Compare actual revenue against budget
- Identify customer and product performance
- Analyse customer activity and churn risk
- Evaluate department and regional performance
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide actionable business recommendations
